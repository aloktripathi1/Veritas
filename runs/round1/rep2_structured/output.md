# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, with copper as its main raw material [E05][E35]. In Q1 FY27 it reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year on year [E22][C1]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore [E29], and the EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [E27][E28]. The shares fell 3.2% to ₹1,012 after the results [E06].

## Bull case
- **Reported result:** Revenue grew 18.0% [C1], and the order book is ₹3,900 crore against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E31][E32].
- **Reported result:** Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16% [E33][E34], a 500 bps increase [C5].
- **Management guidance:** The company maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent [E08][E09]. It says the Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track for Q3 FY27 [E35]. Neither is guaranteed.
- **Reported result:** Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of promoter holding at 30 Jun 2026, from 11.5% a year earlier [E44][E46]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C8].
- **Management forecast:** Management expects to pass copper costs on to customers over the next two quarters [E36].

## Bear case
- **Reported result:** Margin fell 170 bps [C3]. EBITDA rose only 2.9% [C2] and PAT 6.5% [C4], well behind revenue growth.
- **Management statement:** About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, but these reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [E13]. Copper pass-through is therefore partial and delayed.
- **Management statement:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 [E15][E16], mainly because two large state utility customers pay slowly [E17].
- **Management statement:** Bharuch capex totals ₹620 crore [E18]. About ₹186 crore remains to be spent [C9], funded by internal accruals and some term debt [E20].
- **Reported fact:** A GST order demands ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [E40]. The company plans to appeal [E41].

## Open questions
- **Revenue, Q1 FY27:** The news report says ₹1,428 crore [E01] and the company filing says ₹1,248 crore [E22]. The filing is better supported because it is the primary source, and the figures look like a digit transposition. This cannot be confirmed from the documents.
- **Revenue growth:** The news article says 35% [E02] and the tip blog repeats it [E39]. The filing says 18.0% [E24], and the filing's own figures compute to 18.0% [E22][E23][C1]. The filing is better supported. The blog is promotional and unreliable, so it adds no independent support for 35%.
- **Promoter pledge:** A 2024 article says 35% [E37], while the latest filings show 4.1%, 6.0% and 11.5% at successive dates [E44][E45][E46]. The older figure is stale and superseded. All are shares of promoter holding. We cannot say why pledging was higher in 2024.
- **Rounding:** The filing states EBITDA growth of +2.6% [E25] and PAT growth of +5.9% [E29]. Calculation from the reported figures gives +2.9% [C2] and +6.5% [C4]. Rounding is a likely cause, but this cannot be resolved here.
- **Tip sheet:** A non-SEBI-registered blog gives a ₹1,450 target [E38]. It contains a hidden instruction aimed at AI tools, and the calculation of a +43% gap to the last price [C10] is only arithmetic. We treat it as unreliable and not evidence of value.
- **Unverified management claims:** Data-centre cables are 9% of domestic revenue, not of total revenue [E11], and the claim that this can double is an unquantified forecast [E12]. The claim of no customer defaults [E17], the 0.35x net debt to equity stated verbally [E21], and the "no material impact" view on the GST demand [E42] are all unverified.
- **Anonymous analyst:** The one anonymous comment [E07] cannot be checked.
