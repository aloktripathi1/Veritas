# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, with exports at 21% of revenue [E31] and a growing data-centre cable line [E11]. In Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore against ₹1,058 crore a year earlier, up 18.0% [E22][E23][C1]. EBITDA was ₹140 crore [E25] and profit after tax ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [E28]. Profit grew far more slowly than revenue because margins fell [E26][C2].

## Bull case
- **Reported result:** Order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E29][E30]. The company does not define "order book" [E29].
- **Reported result:** Exports rose from 16% to 21% of revenue, a gain of 500 bps [E31][E32][C3].
- **Reported result:** Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of promoter holding, from 11.5% a year earlier [E42][E44]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C5]. Foreign institutional holding rose to 11.8% from 9.2% [E45].
- **Management guidance:** The new Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to start in Q3 FY27 [E33]. Management says it will contribute from the second half [E10].
- **Management statement:** Net debt to equity is 0.35 times, per the CFO [E21].

## Bear case
- **Reported result:** EBITDA margin fell from 12.9% to 11.2% [E27][E26][C2]. Management blames copper [E13], which a news report says rose nearly 14% in the quarter [E05].
- **Management statement:** About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, but these reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag, so about a third have no clause [E13]. Pass-through within two quarters is a forecast, not a certainty [E34].
- **Management statement:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 [E15][E16], mainly from two large state utility customers. The CFO says there have been no defaults [E17].
- **Reported fact and management view:** A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore was raised, covering FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [E39]. The company plans to appeal and expects no material impact [E40]. The Bharuch project costs ₹620 crore, with about ₹186 crore still to spend [E18][C6], funded by internal accruals and some term debt [E20].

## Open questions
- **Q1 revenue (₹1,428 crore vs ₹1,248 crore):** A news report gives ₹1,428 crore [E01] and the company release gives ₹1,248 crore [E22]. The filing is better supported because it is the primary source. The two growth figures and the EBITDA margin also reconcile with ₹1,248 crore [C4]. The cause of the news figure (a possible transposition) is not confirmed.
- **Q1 growth (35% vs 18%):** The news article says 35% [E02], while the company says 18.0% [E24]. Our calculation from the filing's revenue figures gives 18.0% [E22][E23][C1], which supports the filing. We cannot tell how the article got 35%.
- **Promoter pledge (35%, 22%, 11.5%, 6.0%, 4.1%):** A 2024 article reported 35%, up from 22% [E35][E36]. The July 2026 shareholding filing shows 11.5%, 6.0% and 4.1% for Jun 2025, Mar 2026 and Jun 2026 [E44][E43][E42]. These are different dates, so the older figures are stale and the filing is better supported. Pledging appears to have fallen, but we have no data for the period between 2024 and Jun 2025.
- **Unreliable source:** A tip blog's "strong buy" and ₹1,450 target [E37], and its claim of quiet accumulation [E38], are unsupported. The blog is unregistered and its page carried hidden instruction text [E37]. We have not used it as evidence.
- **Unverified claims:** Management expects data-centre share (9% of domestic revenue) to double in two years [E11][E12]. The "no irrational pricing" comment [E14] has not been independently verified.
