# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables for power transmission and data-centre projects, and also exports [E41][E37]. In Q1 FY27 it reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% [C1]. EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [E31][E32], and profit after tax was ₹82 crore [E33]. Shares fell 3.2% to ₹1,012 after the results [E07].

## Bull case
- **Reported result:** The order book was ₹3,900 crore on 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E35][E36].
- **Reported result:** Exports were 21% of revenue, up from 16% [E37][E38].
- **Management guidance:** FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent was maintained [E10][E11]. The Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to capacity, is expected to start commercial production in Q3 FY27 [E39].
- **Forecast:** Management says data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years [E13][E14].
- **Reported/management statement:** Promoter pledge was 4.1% of promoter holding, down from 11.5% a year earlier [E53][E55]. Net debt to equity is 0.35 times per the CFO [E25].

## Bear case
- **Reported result:** Profit growth lagged sales. EBITDA rose 2.9% and profit after tax 6.5% [C2][C4], and margin fell 170 bps [C3]. Management blames mostly copper [E15].
- **Guidance, not result:** Management expects to pass copper costs on over two quarters [E40]. Only about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, and these reset with a lag of one to two quarters [E16].
- **Management statement:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96, mainly from two slow-paying state utilities [E18][E19][E20]. Management says there are no defaults so far [E21].
- **Management statement:** Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore, with ₹186 crore still to spend [C10], funded partly by term debt [E24]. Its contribution depends on the second-half start [E12].
- **Reported fact:** A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore was received [E48]. The company plans to appeal and expects no material impact [E49][E50]. That view is the company's own.

## Open questions
- **Revenue, Q1 FY27 (₹1,428 crore vs ₹1,248 crore):** A news article gives ₹1,428 crore [E01]. The company filing gives ₹1,248 crore [E26]. The filing is better supported: it is the company's own table, and its comparison figure [E27] reproduces the 18.0% growth [C1]. Why the article differs is unknown.
- **Revenue growth (35% vs 18.0%):** The article's 35% [E02] does not match the filing's 18.0% [E28], which is consistent with the filing's own figures [E26][E27]. The article is probably wrong, but this cannot be confirmed.
- **Operating margin:** The article's 11.2% and 170 bps contraction [E04][E05] agree with the filing [E31]. The "conflict" flag appears to come from mixed units, not a real disagreement.
- **Promoter pledge (35% and 22% vs 4.1%, 6.0% and 11.5%):** The 2024 article [E42][E43] is stale. The June 2026 exchange filing [E53][E54][E55] is better supported and more recent. What happened between those dates is not covered.
- **Growth rounding:** The filing states EBITDA +2.6% [E29] and profit +5.9% [E33], while calculation gives +2.9% [C2] and +6.5% [C4]. The likely cause is rounding, but this is not confirmed.
- **Missing or unclear:** The exact basis of the receivable-days comparison is not given [E18]. The copper price rise rests on an unsourced news claim [E06].
- **Promotional material:** A stock-tip blog, admittedly not SEBI registered [E47], is promotional and unsupported [E46]. It was not used as evidence.
