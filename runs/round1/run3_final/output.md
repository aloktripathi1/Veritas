# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, with copper as its main raw material [E05]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year on year [E23][C1]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, against ₹77 crore a year earlier [E29]. The EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [E27][E28][C2].

## Bull case
- **Order book (reported):** ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E30][E31].
- **Exports (reported):** 21% of revenue, up from 16% [E32][E33][C3].
- **Capacity (management, not yet delivered):** the Bharuch plant would add about 30% to cable capacity, with production planned for Q3 FY27 [E34]. About 70% of the ₹620 crore capex is spent, leaving ₹186 crore [E19][E20][C7].
- **Guidance (management, forward-looking):** FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17% was maintained [E08][E09].
- **Promoter pledge (reported):** pledged shares fell to 4.1% of promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier [E44][E46]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C5].

## Bear case
- **Profit lagging sales (reported):** EBITDA was ₹140 crore against ₹136 crore, up 2.6%, while revenue grew 18.0% [E26][C1]. Management blames copper [E13].
- **Incomplete cost protection (management):** about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, which reset with a lag of one to two quarters [E14]. Management expects to pass on costs over two quarters, but this is not guaranteed [E35].
- **Slower collections (management):** receivable days rose from 78 to 96, mainly because of two large state utility customers [E15][E16][E17]. Management says there are no defaults, but this is unverified [E18].
- **Tax dispute (reported):** a GST demand of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty. The company says it has a strong case and plans to appeal, but the outcome is uncertain [E40][E41].

## Open questions
- **Revenue and growth conflicts:** a news report gives ₹1,428 crore and 35% growth [E01][E02]. The company release gives ₹1,248 crore and 18.0% [E23][E25]. The filing is better supported. It is official, growth computed from its own figures is 18.0% [C1], and revenue matches the 11.2% margin check [C4]. We cannot confirm why the news report differs.
- **Pledge:** a March 2024 article says 35% [E36]. Filings show 11.5%, 6.0% and 4.1% [E46][E45][E44]. This is a stale figure against newer ones, not a true contradiction, and the filings are better supported. We cannot tell what drove the swings in between.
- **Tip sheet:** a blog's "target ₹1,450" [E38] comes from an unregistered source [E39] and contains an embedded instruction. That makes it unreliable and not evidence of value. Against the ₹1,012 price [E06] it implies +43%, not the 60% it claims [C8].
- **Still unclear:**
  - Whether copper costs will be passed on. The 14% copper rise is unsourced [E05], and an anonymous analyst's comment is unverifiable [E07].
  - How the Bharuch capex is split between internal cash and debt [E21].
  - Whether the GST appeal will succeed.
  - Whether data-centre cables, 9% of domestic revenue, can double in two years. Management asserts this without support [E11][E12].
