# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, and copper is its main raw material [E05][E11]. In Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% from ₹1,058 crore [E19][E20][C1]. EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% (−170 bps) [E24][E25][C3]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore against ₹77 crore, up 6.5% [E26][E27][C4].

## Bull case
- **Reported:** Order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E28][E29].
- **Reported:** Exports were 21% of revenue, up from 16% (+500 bps) [E30][E31][C5].
- **Reported:** Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier [E42][E44]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C8].
- **Management guidance:** FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17% is maintained [E08]. The Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is expected to start in Q3 FY27 [E32]. About 70% of the ₹620 crore project is spent, or ₹434 crore [E15][E16][C10].
- **Forecast:** Management says data-centre cables, about 9% of domestic revenue, "can double in two years" [E09][E10]. No supporting detail is given.

## Bear case
- **Margins:** EBITDA rose only 2.9% against 18.0% revenue growth [C2][C1]. Management blames mostly copper [E11].
- **Copper pass-through:** About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, which reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [E11]. Management only "expects" to pass on costs over two quarters [E33]. An unnamed analyst flags this as the key watch-point [E07].
- **Working capital:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 [E12][E13], mainly due to two large state utility customers. "No defaults" is management's own assertion [E14].
- **Capex and debt:** ₹186 crore of Bharuch spending remains [C10], funded by internal accruals and some term debt [E17]. Net debt to equity of 0.35x was stated only on the call [E18].
- **Tax dispute:** A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, was disclosed on 2 Sep 2026 [E37]. The company calls its case strong and expects no material impact, but it has only said it intends to appeal. The outcome is uncertain [E38][E39].

## Open questions
- **Revenue conflict:** A news article says ₹1,428 crore [E01]; the company release says ₹1,248 crore [E19]. The release is better supported: it is the primary source, and its year-ago figure reproduces its stated growth [E20][C1]. We cannot confirm why the article differs.
- **Growth conflict:** 35% appears in the same article [E02] and a tip-sheet blog [E36]. The filing says 18.0% [E21], which matches its own figures [C1]. The blog is promotional and unreliable. Its "strong buy" call and ₹1,450 target [E35] are not evidence of value.
- **Pledge figures:** A 2024 article cites 35% [E34]. Filings show 11.5% (Jun 2025), 6.0% (Mar 2026) and 4.1% (Jun 2026) [E44][E43][E42]. The 2024 figure is stale, not contradictory. We cannot tell what happened between 2024 and mid-2025.
- **Rounding mismatch:** The filing states EBITDA growth of +2.6% [E22][E23] and profit growth of +5.9% [E26][E27]. Our calculations give +2.9% and +6.5% [C2][C4]. This is unresolved.
- **Missing information:** There is no detail on order quality and no evidence behind the data-centre doubling. The share price date is unconfirmed [E06], and there is no filing to check net debt [E18].
- **Excluded document:** One document concerned a similarly named but different company and was excluded.
