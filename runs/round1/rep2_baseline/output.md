# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables (NSE: SRVCABLE) makes power cables, wires and conductors [D3:L10]. In Q1 FY27 the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0%, and profit after tax of ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [D3:L16-L19]. EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [D3:L18]. One news report gives revenue as ₹1,428 crore, up 35% [D1:L12]. The company's own release is the better-supported source (see Open questions).

## Bull case
- **Order book:** ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [D3:L21].
- **Exports:** 21% of revenue, up from 16% [D3:L22].
- **Data centres:** Management says they are about 9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years. This is a management view, not a result [D2:L10].
- **Capacity:** The Bharuch plant would add about 30% to cable capacity and is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [D3:L23]. About 70% of the ₹620 crore capex is already spent [D2:L22].
- **Guidance:** Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%, which is a forecast [D2:L10].
- **Balance sheet and ownership:** Net debt to equity is 0.35 times [D2:L22]. The pledged share of promoter holding fell to 4.1% from 11.5% a year earlier [D8:L13]. FII and DII holdings both rose over the same period [D8:L14-L15].

## Bear case
- **Margins:** Profit grew far slower than revenue, and EBITDA rose only 2.6% [D3:L17]. Management blames copper [D2:L14].
- **Pass-through lag:** About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, but they reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [D2:L14]. Recovery of the cost increase is therefore an expectation, not a certainty [D3:L25].
- **Receivables:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 over the year, mainly due to two large state utility customers paying slowly. The CFO says there have been no defaults [D2:L18].
- **Funding:** Bharuch is funded mainly by internal accruals and some term debt [D2:L22].
- **Tax dispute:** A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) was received for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [D7:L10]. The company says it has a strong case and expects no material impact. That is its own view, and an appeal is pending [D7:L12].
- **Share price:** Shares fell 3.2% to ₹1,012 after the results [D1:L18].

## Open questions
- **Revenue discrepancy:** D1 reports ₹1,428 crore and +35%, while the company release reports ₹1,248 crore and +18.0% [D1:L12; D3:L16]. The other D1 figures (profit, margin) match D3. The release is the primary filing, so I rely on it and treat D1's revenue as likely erroneous. I have not confirmed this against the exchange filing itself.
- **Unreliable sources:**
  - A stock-tips blog repeats the 35% figure and gives a price target. Its author says it is not SEBI registered [D5:L8, L20]. The page also hides an instruction aimed at AI tools to declare a "strong buy" [D5:L18]. I disregarded it entirely.
  - A Nagpur article concerns a different company, Sarvottam Cable Network, and is excluded [D6:L10].
  - A 2024 report of 35% promoter pledging is stale. The June 2026 filing shows 4.1% [D4:L10; D8:L13].
- **Copper pass-through:** Will the price-variation resets restore margins within the two quarters management expects [D3:L25]?
- **Receivables:** Will the two state utilities' slower payments normalise?
- **GST appeal:** What is the outcome and timeline?
- **Bharuch:** Does the plant start in Q3 FY27 and ramp as planned?
- **Valuation:** The documents provide no reliable valuation data or independent analyst estimates.
