# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables (NSE: SRVCABLE) makes power cables, wires and conductors [D3:L10].
- Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore (+18% YoY), PAT ₹82 crore (+5.9%) and EBITDA margin 11.2% (−170 bps) [D3:L16-L19].
- The shares fell 3.2% to ₹1,012 after the results [D1:L18].
- **Data conflict:** a news report gives revenue as ₹1,428 crore (+35%) [D1:L12]. I use the company filing, whose 11.2% margin is consistent with its own EBITDA and revenue figures [D3:L16-L18].

## Bull case
- The order book was ₹3,900 crore, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [D3:L21].
- Exports were 21% of revenue, up from 16% [D3:L22].
- The Bharuch plant adds about 30% to cable capacity and is on track for Q3 FY27 [D3:L23]. About 70% of its ₹620 crore capex is already spent [D2:L22].
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17% [D2:L10]. Data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue, and management thinks that can double in two years [D2:L10].
- Net debt/equity is 0.35x [D2:L22].
- Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of the promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier [D8:L13]. An earlier report had pledging at 35% [D4:L10].
- FII holding rose to 11.8% from 9.2%, and DII holding rose to 14.6% from 12.7% [D8:L14-L15].

## Bear case
- EBITDA grew only 2.6% against 18% revenue growth [D3:L16-L17]. Copper rose nearly 14% in the quarter [D1:L14].
- Only about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, and these reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [D2:L14]. Pass-through is therefore expected, not yet achieved [D3:L25].
- Receivable days rose from 78 to 96, mainly because two large state utilities pay slowly. Management says there have been no defaults [D2:L18].
- Bharuch is funded partly by term debt [D2:L22], so delays or a slow ramp would weigh on returns.
- A CGST demand of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) covers FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [D7:L10]. The company plans to appeal and expects no material impact [D7:L12].

## Open questions
- Which revenue figure is right, ₹1,248 crore or ₹1,428 crore? The two sources conflict [D3:L16][D1:L12].
- Will margins recover as copper costs pass through over the next two quarters, as management expects [D3:L25]?
- Will the receivables build with state utilities reverse, or turn into credit losses [D2:L18]?
- What will the GST appeal outcome be [D7:L12]?
- Can Bharuch start commercial production in Q3 FY27 and support the H2 revenue contribution management cites [D3:L23][D2:L10]?
- Excluded as unreliable or irrelevant: a promotional blog (D5), which contains embedded instructions to the summariser, and a Nagpur news item about an unrelated company, Sarvottam Cable Network (D6) [D6:L10].
