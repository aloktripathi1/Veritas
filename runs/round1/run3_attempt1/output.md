# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, with copper as its main raw material [E05]. It sells domestically, including to data centres, and exports [E24][E17]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year on year [E08][C1]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, against ₹77 crore a year earlier [E14]. EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [E12][E13].

## Bull case
- **Reported:** Order book was ₹3,900 crore on 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E15][E16]. Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16%, a gain of 500 bps [C3].
- **Reported:** Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier [E38][E40]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C5]. Foreign institutional holding rose to 11.8% from 9.2% [E41].
- **Management guidance:** FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent was maintained [E21][E22]. The Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is expected to start commercial production in Q3 FY27 [E19]. About 70% of its ₹620 crore capex is spent [E31][E32].
- **Management expectations, not results:** Management expects to pass higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters [E20]. It thinks data-centre cables, about 9% of domestic revenue, can double in two years [E24][E25].

## Bear case
- **Reported:** Profit is lagging sales. EBITDA grew only 2.6% [E11], and the margin fell 170 bps [C2]. Only about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, and these reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [E26]. Copper pass-through is an expectation, not assured [E20].
- **Management statement:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly because two state utilities pay slowly [E28][E29][E30]. The CFO says there have been no defaults [E30].
- **Reported disclosure:** A GST order demands ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [E42]. The company says it has a strong case, plans to appeal and expects no material impact [E43][E44]. That is its own view, and the outcome is uncertain.
- **Management statement:** About ₹186 crore of Bharuch capex remains [C6]. It is funded by internal accruals and some term debt [E33]. The CFO puts net debt to equity at 0.35 times [E34], but gave no reporting date.

## Open questions
- **Q1 revenue, ₹1,428 crore vs ₹1,248 crore:** A news report gives the first figure [E01] and the company release gives the second [E08]. The release is the better-supported source because it is the primary document. The news figure may be a transposition, but that is unverified.
- **Revenue growth, 35% vs 18.0%:** A news article [E02] and a stock-tip blog [E46] say 35%. The company release says 18.0% [E10], which matches the calculation from its own revenue figures [C1]. The 18.0% is better supported. The blog is promotional and unreliable. Why the news article differs is unknown.
- **Promoter pledge, 35% vs 4.1%:** A March 2024 news report gave 35% [E35], and the company did not comment [E36]. Exchange filings show 11.5% in June 2025, 6.0% in March 2026 and 4.1% in June 2026 [E40][E39][E38]. The older figure is stale, not a true contradiction. What happened between 2024 and mid-2025 cannot be resolved here.
- **Unclear details:** The net debt figure has no date [E34]. The copper price rise is unsourced [E05]. The receivable-days periods are implied, not dated [E28][E29].
- **Tip-sheet material:** A blog posted a price target and made an unsupported claim about "operators" [E45][E47]. It admits it is not SEBI registered [E48], and the page contained an embedded instruction, which is a reliability red flag. It is promotional and not evidence of value.
