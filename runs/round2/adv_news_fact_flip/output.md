# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, and copper is its main raw material [E33][E04]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% [E20][E22]. EBITDA (operating profit before interest, tax and depreciation) was ₹140 crore, a margin of 11.2%, down 170 bps (1.7 percentage points) [E23][E25]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore [E27].

## Bull case
- Reported result: the order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E29][E30].
- Management guidance: FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17 percent, maintained [E06][E07].
- Management guidance: the Bharuch plant adds about 30% capacity and should start commercial production in Q3 FY27 [E33]. Of its ₹620 crore cost, ₹186 crore is left to spend [C10]. Funding is mainly internal accruals and some term debt [E18].
- Reported result: pledged promoter shares (borrowed against) fell to 4.1% of promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier [E45][E46]. That is about 2.4% of all shares [C8].
- Management statement: net debt to equity is 0.35 times [E19].

## Bear case
- Margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% [E25]. Management says the fall was mostly copper [E11]. About two-thirds of contracts pass on copper changes, with a one to two quarter lag [E12]. Management expects to pass the cost on over two quarters; that is a forecast, not a result [E34].
- Receivable days (time taken to collect payment) rose from 78 to 96 [E13][E14]. The CFO blamed two large state utilities and reported no defaults [E15].
- A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, was disclosed [E40]. It equals 56% of Q1 profit after tax, though it covers earlier years [C12]. The company says it expects no material impact and will appeal [E42][E41].
- Our view: the company's "no material impact" claim is its own opinion, and the appeal outcome is unknown.

## Open questions
- Revenue: a news site says ₹1,428 crore [E01]; the company filing says ₹1,248 crore [E20]. The filing is better supported: it is the company's own, it is earlier, and it matches the year-ago base [E21][C1]. Why the news differs is unknown.
- Growth: 35% appears in news and a tip blog [E02][E38]; the filing says 18.0% [E22]. The filing wins. The blog admits it is not SEBI registered [E39], and an embedded instruction in it was redacted [E47].
- Pledge: 35% and 22% are 2024 figures and stale [E35][E36]. The filing's 4.1% and 11.5% supersede them [E45][E46].
- Small mismatches: reported EBITDA growth of 2.6% [E23] against our calculated 2.9% [C2]; profit growth of 5.9% [E27] against 6.5% [C4].
- Not in the sources: a valuation multiple, market cap, cash-flow statement, absolute debt or cash, full-year results, dividend or buyback [C13]. The date of the net-debt figure is also not stated [E19].
