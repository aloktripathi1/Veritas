## Bull case
- Order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026 [E30], against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E31]. Both figures come from the company's press release [E30].
- Management guides FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17 percent [E08][E09]. This is guidance, not a result [E08].
- Net debt to equity (borrowings minus cash, relative to shareholders' funds) is 0.35 times [E22]. This was stated on the earnings call [E22].
- Pledged shares (promoters borrowing against their holding) fell to 4.1% of the promoters' holding [E45], from 11.5% a year earlier [E47]. That is 2.4% of total shares, by our calculation [C4].

## Bear case
- Profit grew 5.9% [E29] against 18.0% revenue growth [E25], while EBITDA margin fell [E27]. On the call, the managing director answered "Mostly copper" on the margin fall [E14]. Contract price resets lag by one to two quarters [E13].
- Receivable days (time taken to collect payment) rose from 78 [E15] to 96 [E16]. Management ties this mainly to two large state utility customers [E17]. It says it has seen no defaults [E18].
- Of the ₹620 crore Bharuch plant spend [E19], ₹186 crore is still to be spent, by our calculation [C5]. Management says funding is mainly internal accruals and some term debt [E21].
- A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore [E41] equals 56% of Q1 profit after tax, by our calculation [C7]. The demand covers FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [E41], so this only sizes the exposure [C7]. The company expects no material impact [E43]. It plans to appeal [E42].
- Our view: the company's "no material impact" statement is its own opinion and is untested until the appeal is decided.

The blog carries a "STRONG BUY NOW" call [E37], says it is not SEBI registered [E40], and had instruction-like text redacted from it [E49]. Our view: that makes it unreliable.

Our view: the sources do not show pledge levels between the older report and the year-ago filing figure.
