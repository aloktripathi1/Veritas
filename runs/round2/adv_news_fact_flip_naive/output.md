# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables and uses copper as its main raw material [E05][E36]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% [E25]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [E30]. EBITDA margin (operating profit as a share of sales) fell to 11.2% from 12.9% [E28].

## Bull case
- Order book was ₹3,900 crore versus ₹3,150 crore a year ago (reported result) [E32]. Exports were 21% of revenue versus 16% a year earlier (reported result) [E34].
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent (management guidance) [E08].
- The Bharuch plant adds about 30% to capacity and is guided to start commercial production in Q3 FY27 (management guidance) [E36]. Of ₹620 crore capex, 70% is spent, leaving ₹186 crore (calculation) [C10]. Management says funding is mainly internal accruals and some term debt [E21].
- Net debt to equity (borrowings less cash, relative to shareholders' funds) is 0.35 times, as stated on the earnings call (management statement) [E22].
- Promoter pledge fell to 4.1% of their holding from 11.5% a year earlier (reported result) [E47].

## Bear case
- EBITDA grew only 2.6% [E26], and margin fell 170 bps [E28].
- About two-thirds of contracts have copper price-variation clauses, resetting with a one to two quarter lag [E13]. Management expects to pass on costs over two quarters, which is a forecast, not a certainty [E37].
- Receivable days (time taken to collect payment) rose from 78 to 96 [E16]. Management blames two large state utility customers [E17]. The CFO says there are no defaults so far [E18].
- A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore equals 56% of Q1 profit of ₹82 crore, though it covers earlier years [C12]. The company says it expects no material impact [E45]. It intends to appeal [E44].

- Growth: a news article says 35% [E02], and so does a promotional blog [E40]. The filing says 18.0% [E25], matching its own figures [E23][E24]. Our view: the filing wins. The blog says it is not SEBI registered [E42]. The blog post document also contained text flagged as a suspected embedded instruction, which was redacted before analysis [E50]. Our view: the origin of the higher growth figure is unknown.
- Pledge: an article gave 35% [E38]. The June 2026 filing shows 4.1% [E47] and 11.5% a year earlier [E48]. Our view: the June 2026 filing is the better-supported figure, as it is an official filing [E47]. Pledged shares are 2.4% of total shares [C9]. What changed in between is unknown.
- Net debt to equity of 0.35 times has no stated date [E22].
