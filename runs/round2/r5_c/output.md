# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, and copper is its main raw material [E06]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% on a year earlier [E23][E25]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [E30]. Operating margin (EBITDA, profit before interest, tax and depreciation, as a share of revenue) fell 170 bps (1.7 percentage points) to 11.2% [E28].

## Bull case
- Order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier (reported) [E32][E33].
- Exports were 21% of revenue, against 16% a year earlier (reported) [E34][E35].
- Management guides FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17 percent (guidance, not a result) [E09][E10]. The new Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track for Q3 FY27 output, says the company [E36].
- Promoters' pledged shares (shares borrowed against) fell to 4.1% of the promoters' holding, from 11.5% a year earlier (reported) [E48][E49]. That is about 2.4% of total shares [C8].
- Net debt to equity (debt after cash, versus shareholders' money) is 0.35 times, per the CFO (management statement) [E22].

## Bear case
- Operating profit (EBITDA) rose only 2.6%, against revenue growth of 18.0% [E26][E25]. Management blames copper; about two-thirds of contracts pass on price changes with a one-to-two-quarter lag [E14].
- Receivable days (time taken to collect payment) rose from 78 to 96 [E16][E17]. Management blames two state utility customers and reports no defaults [E18].
- A ₹46.3 crore GST demand (indirect-tax claim) covers FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [E44]. That equals 56% of Q1 FY27 profit, though that profit is for one quarter only [C11]. The company expects no material impact and plans to appeal [E46][E45]. Our view: this is the company's own assessment, not independently checked.
- Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore, with 186 crore still to spend [E19][C9]. It is funded mainly by internal accruals and some term debt, with no split given [E21].

## Open questions
- Revenue: news says ₹1,428 crore [E01]; the company release says ₹1,248 crore [E23].
- Growth: news says 35% [E02]; the release says 18.0% [E25]; a promotional blog repeats 35% [E41]. Year-ago revenue of ₹1,058 crore [E24] gives 18.0% [C1]. Our view: the filing is better supported, as the company's primary document. Why the news differs is unresolved.
- Pledge: a 2024 article said 35% of promoter holding [E38]; the latest filing says 4.1% [E48], from 11.5% a year ago [E49]. Our view: the older figure is stale; the newer filing is better supported.
- Reported profit growth of 5.9% [E30] differs slightly from our calculated 6.5% [C4]; EBITDA 2.6% [E26] versus 2.9% [C2].
- Data check: the sources lack a valuation multiple, market cap, cash-flow statement, absolute debt or cash, full-year results, dividend or buyback [C12].
- A tip blog (unregistered, promotional) was not used as evidence [E43][E51].
