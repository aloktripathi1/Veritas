# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
The company makes cables [E33], and copper is its main raw material [E03]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% [E20][E22]. EBITDA (operating profit before depreciation and interest) was ₹140 crore, with a margin of 11.2% [E23][E25]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [E27].

## Bull case
- Reported: the order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026 [E29], up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E30].
- Reported: exports were 21% of revenue, against 16% a year earlier [E31][E32].
- Guidance: management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent [E05][E06].
- Management statement: the Bharuch plant adds about 30% capacity, with production due in Q3 FY27 [E33]. Of the ₹620 crore capex [E16], 186 crore remains to be spent [C10]. It is funded mainly by internal accruals and some term debt [E18].
- Management says net debt to equity (net borrowings against shareholders' funds) is 0.35 times [E19]. Reported: pledged promoter shares were 4.1% of promoter holding at 30 June 2026 [E42], against 6.0% at 31 March 2026 [E43] and 11.5% at 30 June 2025 [E44].

## Bear case
- Reported: EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% [E25]. EBITDA rose only 2.6% [E23] against 18.0% revenue growth [E22]. Management says a sharp rise in copper prices affected margins [E34]. The call excerpt also records the reply "Mostly copper." [E10]. Its contract price clauses reset with a one to two quarter lag [E11]. It expects to pass costs on over two quarters, which is a forecast [E34].
- Receivable days (time taken to collect payment) rose from 78 to 96 [E12][E13]. Management says mainly two large state utilities are paying slower [E14], with no defaults so far [E15].
- A GST demand of ₹46.3 crore [E38] equals 56% of Q1 profit after tax, and the demand covers earlier years [C12]. The company says it expects no material impact [E40] and plans to appeal [E39]. Our view: that is its own assessment and is untested.

Why the news figures differ cannot be resolved.
- Pledge: a news article says 35% of promoter holding is pledged, up from 22% six months earlier [E35]. The June 2026 filing says 4.1% [E42], versus 6.0% [E43] and 11.5% [E44] in its earlier columns. Our view: the article is older than the filing, and the filing is official, so the news figure looks stale [E35][E42]. Whether it was true then cannot be checked.
