# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables, and copper is its main raw material [E05]. In Q1 FY27, revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% from a year earlier, per the company's release [E22][E24]. The operating margin (EBITDA margin: profit before interest, depreciation and tax as a share of sales) fell 170 bps (1.7 percentage points) to 11.2% [E26]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore [E28].

## Bull case
- **Order book (reported):** It was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [E29][E30].
- **Exports (reported):** Exports were 21% of revenue, against 16% a year earlier [E31][E32]. That is a rise of 500 bps [C3].
- **Profit and guidance:** Profit after tax rose to ₹82 crore from ₹77 crore (reported) [E28]. Management guides to FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17 percent, which is guidance, not a result [E08].
- **Bharuch plant (management statement):** The plant adds about 30% to cable capacity and is said to start production in Q3 FY27 [E33]. Capex is ₹620 crore in total, with ₹186 crore left to spend [C6]. It is funded mainly through internal accruals and some term debt [E20].
- **Debt and pledging:** Management says net debt to equity is 0.35 times [E21]. Promoters' pledged shares (borrowed against) fell to 4.1% of their holding from 11.5% a year earlier [E44][E45]. That is 2.4% of total shares [C5].

## Bear case
- **Margin squeeze:** EBITDA rose only 2.6%, to ₹140 crore [E25]. The reporter says copper rose nearly 14% in the quarter [E05]. About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, but they reset with a lag of one to two quarters [E12]. Management expects to pass on the costs over two quarters, which is a forecast, not a promise [E34].
- **Slower collections:** Receivable days (time taken to collect from customers) rose from 78 to 96 [E14][E15]. The CFO blames two large state utility customers [E16]. The CFO says there have been no defaults, which is unverified [E17].
- **GST demand:** A tax demand of ₹46.3 crore, covering earlier years, was disclosed on 2 September 2026 [E40]. That equals 56% of Q1 profit, which sizes the exposure only [C8]. The company says it does not expect any material impact and intends to appeal [E42][E41]. This is the company's own view, and the appeal is not yet filed [E41].
- **Competition:** Management says it sees no irrational pricing from competitors, which cannot be verified [E13].

## Open questions
- **Revenue conflict:** A news report says Q1 revenue was ₹1,428 crore [E01]. The company release says ₹1,248 crore [E22]. The filing is better supported, because it is the company's own document and its year-ago base gives 18.0% [C1]. Why the news figure differs is unknown.
- **Growth conflict:** A news report gives 35% growth [E02]. A tip blog repeats it [E38]. The company reports 18.0% [E24], which matches its year-ago figure of ₹1,058 crore [E23]. We trust the filing, and the blog is promotional.
- **Pledge conflict:** A 2024 article cited 35% pledged [E35]. The June 2026 filing shows 4.1% [E44], and 11.5% a year earlier [E45]. The old figure is stale, not a contradiction.
- **Missing data:** The sources have no valuation multiple, market cap, cash-flow statement, absolute debt or cash figure, full-year results, or dividend or buyback information [C9]. The net-debt figure has no date [E21]. A blog's ₹1,450 price target is promotional and not evidence [E37].
