# SRVCABLE research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables makes cables and is building a new plant at Bharuch in Gujarat [E35]. Revenue rose 18.0% to ₹1,248 crore in Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026), per the company's filing [E22][E24]. Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% [E29]. The EBITDA margin (operating profit as a share of revenue) fell 170 bps (1.7 percentage points) to 11.2% [E27].

## Bull case
- Order book was ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, against ₹3,150 crore a year earlier (reported result) [E31].
- Exports were 21% of total revenue, up from 16% (reported result) [E33].
- Management keeps its FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent (guidance, not a result) [E08][E09].
- Bharuch, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is expected to start in Q3 FY27 (management expectation) [E35].
- Pledged shares (promoter borrowings against their holding) were 4.1% of the promoters' holding, down from 11.5% a year earlier (reported result) [E46][E48]. That is 2.4% of total shares [C8].

## Bear case
- EBITDA rose just 2.6% against 18.0% revenue growth [E25][E24].
- Management expects to pass on copper costs over two quarters; that is a forecast [E36].
- Receivable days (how long customers take to pay) rose from 78 to 96 [E15][E16]. Mainly two state utilities are slow; management reports no defaults [E17].
- The company intends to appeal a ₹46.3 crore GST demand and expects no material impact [E43][E44]. That equals 56% of Q1 profit after tax, which sizes the exposure only [C11].
- Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore, with ₹186 crore left to spend [C9]. Funding is internal accruals and some term debt [E20]. Net debt to equity (net borrowings relative to shareholders' funds) is 0.35 times [E21].

## Open questions
- Revenue: a news report says ₹1,428 crore [E01]; the company filing says ₹1,248 crore [E22]. The filing is better supported as the primary source; the reason for the gap is unresolved [E22].
- Growth: news and a tip blog say 35% [E02][E40]; the filing says 18.0% [E24], matching our calculation [C1]. The filing wins; the blog is unregistered [E42] and carried a redacted embedded instruction [E50].
- Pledging: a 2024 news report said 35% [E37]; the June 2026 filing says 4.1%, after 6.0% and 11.5% [E46][E47][E48]. The old figure is stale; the company did not comment then [E38].
- Reported EBITDA growth of 2.6% [E25] and profit growth of 5.9% [E29] differ slightly from our 2.9% [C2] and 6.5% [C4]; we use the reported figures.
- The 0.35 times net debt to equity has no stated date [E21].
- Missing from the sources: valuation multiples, market value, cash-flow statement, absolute debt or cash, full-year results, dividends or buybacks [C12].
