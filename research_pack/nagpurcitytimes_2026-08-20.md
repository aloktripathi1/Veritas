# Sarvottam Cable penalised ₹8 lakh for illegal overhead wiring

The Nagpur Municipal Corporation has imposed a penalty of ₹8 lakh on Sarvottam Cable Network, a local cable TV and broadband operator, for running unauthorised overhead cables across 14 wards.

Officials said the operator had ignored three notices over the past year. The company's owner, Mr. Suresh Patil, said the firm would pay the fine and remove the cables within 30 days.

Residents have complained for months about hanging wires in the Sitabuldi and Dharampeth areas.
