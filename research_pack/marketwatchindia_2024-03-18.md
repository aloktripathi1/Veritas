# Promoter pledge at Sarvottam Cables rises to 35%, raising governance concerns

The promoters of Sarvottam Cables Ltd have increased the share of their holding that is pledged with lenders to 35%, up from 22% six months ago, according to exchange data.

High promoter pledging is often seen as a risk for minority shareholders, because lenders can sell the pledged shares if the stock price falls sharply.

The company did not respond to a request for comment.
