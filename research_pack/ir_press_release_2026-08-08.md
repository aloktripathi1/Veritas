# Sarvottam Cables Ltd announces Q1 FY27 results

**Mumbai, 8 August 2026.** Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE), a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors, today announced results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

**Consolidated highlights (Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26)**

| ₹ crore | Q1 FY27 | Q1 FY26 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations | 1,248 | 1,058 | +18.0% |
| EBITDA | 140 | 136 | +2.6% |
| EBITDA margin | 11.2% | 12.9% | −170 bps |
| Profit after tax | 82 | 77 | +5.9% |

- Order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago.
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue (Q1 FY26: 16%).
- The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27.

Commenting on the results, Managing Director Mr. Anil Deshpande said: "Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains strong. Margins were affected by a sharp rise in copper prices during the quarter, which we expect to pass on to customers over the next two quarters."
