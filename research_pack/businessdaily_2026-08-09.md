# Sarvottam Cables' Q1 revenue jumps 35%, but copper costs bite into margins

Sarvottam Cables Ltd reported a sharp rise in revenue for the April–June quarter, driven by orders from power utilities and data-centre developers.

Revenue from operations rose to **₹1,428 crore**, a 35% increase from the year-ago period, the company said in an exchange filing on Friday. Net profit rose about 6% to ₹82 crore.

Operating margins, however, contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% as copper, the company's main raw material, rose nearly 14% during the quarter.

"The volume story is intact, but investors will watch whether the company can pass on higher copper costs," said a Mumbai-based analyst who did not wish to be named.

Shares of the company fell 3.2% to ₹1,012 on the NSE on Friday after the results.
