# Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations: receipt of GST demand order

Sarvottam Cables Ltd hereby informs that it has received an order from the office of the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, raising a demand of **₹46.3 crore** (including interest and penalty) relating to input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22.

The Company believes it has a strong case on merits, based on legal advice, and intends to file an appeal before the appropriate authority. The Company does not expect any material impact on its financial position or operations.
