# Q1 FY27 earnings conference call: excerpt

**Anil Deshpande (MD):** For the full year FY27 we are maintaining our revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. Bharuch will contribute from the second half. Data-centre cables are now about 9 percent of our domestic revenue, and we think that can double in two years.

**Analyst (Rohan M., a brokerage):** Margins are down 170 basis points. How much of this is copper, and how much is competitive pricing?

**Anil Deshpande (MD):** Mostly copper. About two-thirds of our contracts have price-variation clauses, but they reset with a lag of one to two quarters. We are not seeing irrational pricing from competitors.

**Analyst (Priya S., a mutual fund):** Your receivable days seem to have gone up. Can you comment?

**Meera Iyer (CFO):** Yes, receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year. That is mainly two large state utility customers, where payments are slower. We are monitoring this closely and have not seen any defaults.

**Analyst (Priya S.):** And capex?

**Meera Iyer (CFO):** Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore in total, about 70 percent already spent. It is funded mainly through internal accruals and some term debt. Net debt to equity is 0.35 times.
